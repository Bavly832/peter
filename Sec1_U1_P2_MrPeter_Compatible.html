Language — The Past Continuous Tense

الماضي المستمر، علاقته بالماضي البسيط، الروابط الزمنية، الاختصار، المبني للمجهول، والأفعال التي لا تستخدم عادةً في المستمر.

Form & Core Rule

Subject + was / were + V-ing

Time Expressions

whilewhenasjust asall day / all night / ...

Usage

Past Continuous + Past Simple Patterns

While / As / Just as / When + Past Continuous → Past Simple
While he was playing the game, it rained.
Past Simple + while / as / just as / when + Past Continuous
The phone rang while he was studying his lessons.
Past Continuous + while / as / just as / when + Past Continuous
As I was studying my lessons, my sister was watching TV.
